# Match analysis report: reconstructed matches (13 October 2026)

All analysis comes from the existing Match Insights Engine (`backend/pipeline.py`) run on each reconstructed file. The per-match reports are generated by `backend/analysis_report.py` into `docs/match_reports/<id>.md`, with machine-readable copies in `data/eval/match_reports/<id>.json`.

Every evidence item is tagged with its basis:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ground_truth positions` | DFL in-stadium tracking |
| `recorded_event` | Official DFL feed, or SkillCorner's computer-vision event feed (not official) |
| `observed + model_inferred positions` | Broadcast detections plus our hidden-player estimates |
| `heuristic over …` | Tactical labels, detectors and the narrative |

Moments are linked by order in time ("preceded", "was followed by"), never by proven cause.

**Total: 30 matches, analysed in 32 catalog entries**, because each legacy-EPL half is its own entry. All 32 analysis runs succeeded; `data/eval/match_reports/index.json` lists any failures and there are none. The synthetic test match and the earlier trial7h candidates were left unchanged and are not re-analysed here.

Notes on the inputs:
- **No match was reconstructed from video and also scored against truth.** That data does not exist (see `dataset_inventory.md`).
- The A-League matches are reconstructed from **SkillCorner's** broadcast detections, not from video processed by us.
- Only the Arsenal clip comes from our own video pipeline.

**Quality checks** (`data/eval/export_quality_r13.json`, 59 files): 0 issues.
- Timestamps are continuous.
- No estimated position lies off the pitch.
- No estimated step is faster than 10 m/s.
- Measured positions are identical in the live and replay views.
- 32 files contain a few *measured or ground-truth* positions more than 3 m outside the lines (players by the benches). These are kept as measured.

**Fixes made during validation:**
1. SkillCorner event coordinates are normalised to each event's attacking direction. Undoing that per event brought the event-to-tracking distance from 32.8 m to a median of 0.4–0.7 m.
2. Provider scores are cumulative over the match, so the second half must start from the first-half score.
3. Goals are checked against the ball tracking. The provider's score fields change before or long after the goal, so a goal is credited to the shot after which the tracked ball enters that goal, before any later shot; the goal is timed at the ball's entry. Previously a blocked or saved shot could be credited when the rebound went in. For example, in `skc-1886347` M. Mata's shot came back out, the goal was F. De Vries' rebound, and the old attribution showed the ball bouncing off while the goal counted. Result over 65 goals:
   - 47: ball tracked entering the goal;
   - 16: ball not seen entering the goal (tracking lost), labelled unverified, with lower moment confidence;
   - 2: no shot and no tracked entry, so time and scorer are unknown.
4. During replays and close-ups the provider lists nobody (30,335 of 118,255 seconds, 26 %). Players now stay on the pitch through those gaps, according to the match sheet, and are estimated with growing uncertainty.

5. Formation-shift moments were stamped one minute before the new shape was read. The detector takes "after" from the next minute, so the moment contradicted the per-minute Shape panel (for example "switch to 4-4-2" at 44:00 while 44' still read 4-2-3-1). Moments are now stamped at the first minute showing the new shape; all 39 formation-shift moments agree with the per-minute shape.

After these fixes the goal events reproduce the official final score in **19 / 20** A-League matches. In `skc-1899585`, one home goal never appears in the provider event stream. It is flagged on the lobby card and in the report, and no goal event was invented for it.

## 2. Does the analysis survive reconstruction? (DFL, where truth exists)

For each DFL half, the engine was run twice: on ground truth, and on the camera reconstruction with ~75 % of player-seconds hidden. The tables compare the two readings.

**Per minute:**

| Quantity | Mean absolute difference, reconstruction vs truth (14 halves) |
|---|---|
| Defensive line | **1.7 m** |
| Team width | 3.4 m |
| Compactness | 1.2 m |
| Depth | 2.7 m |
| Press index (heuristic, 0–100) | 4.1 |
| Formation label (template fit), agreement per team-minute | **57 %** on average, ranging from 14 % to 85 % by half |

**Position-based moments** (tactical turning points, formation shifts, counters):

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Moments found on truth | 43 |
| Moments found on reconstruction | 39 |
| Truth moments recalled within ±120 s | 29 (67 %) |
| Reconstruction-only moments | 10 |

**At the 125 key moments**, averaged over ±5 s, with 2.2 of 10 outfield players observed on average:

| Quantity | Camera only | Reconstructed |
|---|---|---|
| Defensive line error vs truth | 11.6 m, and computable at only 72 / 125 moments | **3.3 m**, computable at 125 / 125 |
| Width error vs truth | 15.6 m | **7.1 m** |
| Players within 15 m of the ball | 0.46 | 0.38 |

Conclusions:
- **Team-level geometry** (line, width, compactness) from reconstructed positions is reliable to a few metres, and much better than using only what the camera shows.
- **Formation labels and position-based moments are not reliable** from reconstruction. Formation labels are already fragile on truth, because the template fit is unstable between similar shapes. A third of truth moments are missed and a quarter of reconstruction moments are spurious.

**Rule applied in the reports:** formations and tactical-shift moments on broadcast-derived matches are shown as *experimental*. Their confidence is scaled down by visibility through the engine's coverage factor.

The 6.8 m width error in an earlier session used soft event pins that leak future information into live estimates. They are off here, so the two figures are not directly comparable.

### Example evidence chains

These are taken verbatim from the generated reports.

**Sydney FC vs Adelaide United (`skc-1996435`), 66': "Sydney FC switch to 3-5-2".** FORMATION_SHIFT; confidence 0.71; visibility reliability 0.44, meaning 5 of 11 players observed on average.

| Basis | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Heuristic over observed + model-inferred positions | Shape changed 4-1-4-1 → 3-5-2 |
| Observed + model-inferred positions | Defensive line +34.2 m, width −0.4 m |
| Observed + model-inferred positions | Territory 62.0 % → 65.3 % |
| Recorded event (provider) | Goal at 74:34 |

The goal *followed* the shape change. The data does not show that the change caused it.

The +34 m line change is implausibly large for a real tactical adjustment. With only 5 of 11 players seen, it is more consistent with an estimation swing. **This chain should be treated as a hypothesis.** It shows exactly why these moments are labelled experimental.

**Fortuna Düsseldorf goal (`dfl-J03WR9-r13`, 1st half, 14'): M. Karbownik.** GOAL_BUILD_UP; recorded event; positions are ground truth. The camera estimate at that moment:

|---|---|---|---|
| Defensive line | 80.3 m | 72.2 m | 70.7 m |
| Width | 28.1 m | 47.4 m | 41.7 m |

Reconstruction reduces the line error from 9.6 m to 1.5 m and the width error from 13.6 m to 5.7 m.

## 5. What is supported by what

| Conclusion type | Supported by | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Goals, substitutions, cards (DFL) | Official event feed | Fact |
| Goals, shots, passes (A-League) | SkillCorner CV events; goals checked against official scores (19 / 20) | Provider fact, not official |
| Team line, width, compactness (DFL) | Ground truth | Fact (heuristic definitions) |
| Team line, width, compactness (A-League, EPL, Arsenal) | Detections + model estimates | Estimate. Expect ≈ 2–4 m per-minute error (DFL check, §2). |
| Formation labels, tactical shifts, press spells | Heuristic detectors | Experimental. 57 % formation agreement with truth under reconstruction; high press unvalidated on real football. |
| Individual player claims for unseen players | Model estimates | **Not supported.** Do not use. |
| "X caused Y" | None | Never claimed. Only "preceded" or "followed". |
| What-if counterfactuals | Simulator only | Not available for real matches |

## 6. Limitations

- Engine detectors were tuned on synthetic and DFL data. A-League tactical moments have not been validated against any labels.
- SkillCorner identities and detections are the provider's and have not been verified independently. 3 goals have no located shot.
- During replays and close-ups (about a quarter of A-League seconds: 30,335 of 118,255), every position is an estimate. These intervals appear in the reports as unreliable or abstained minutes.
- The live views of broadcast-derived matches are past-only reconstructions of a recorded match. They are not live data feeds.
